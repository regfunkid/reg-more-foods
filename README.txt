Hi! Thanks for downloading (or installing!) Reg's More Foods.

Throughout this project's folders and files, you will find text files just like this one.
They'll simply be named "info.txt", and you can open one up to get more information about whatever it is you may be seeing.
(For example, the "version number" file in this folder simply states what version this pack is in. "CHANGELOG.md" is where you can find the list of updates!)

I realized that many people who are new to data packs may (or will) be confused about what the heck they're looking at when they open one of these up, sort of like new car owners popping the hood of their car and being greeted with several doohickeys and thingamabobs.

The purpose of these text files is to show new creators what the point of a folder or file may be, why the files may be named strangely, or other important information they may need or want to know.
The goal is for me to be able to teach people my thought process for this project, so they can create their own add-ons for it--or better yet, be inspired by what I create to make their own projects!

If you ever have ANY questions whatsoever about this project, shoot me a DM.
In the event that one or neither of these options are available, you can always read the Wiki for more information.
